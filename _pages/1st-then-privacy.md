# 1st Then Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 17, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 17, 2026

1st Then is a visual routine app made by Margo Studios. Parents build routines with pictures, and children tap the pictures to check off steps. This policy explains what the app does with information.

**The short version: 1st Then does not collect your information. Everything you make in the app stays on your device.**

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect any information.** We do not receive, store, or have access to anything you enter or create in 1st Then.

- No account, sign-up, or login is needed.
- The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal details.
- The app does not contain analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking tools.
- The app does not connect to the internet. The Android version does not even request the internet permission.

---

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The things you create in the app are saved only on your own phone or tablet:

- **Routines and steps:** names, pictures, colors, timers, and how many times each step is repeated
- **Progress:** which steps have been checked off today
- **Settings:** such as how finished steps look
- **Your own pictures:** if you choose to use a photo for a step, a copy of that picture is saved in the app's private storage on your device

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, recover it, or delete it for you.

---

## 3. Camera and Photos

The camera and photo library are used only if you choose to add your own picture to a step.

- **Camera:** the app asks for camera permission only when you tap "Take a photo." The picture is saved inside the app on your device.
- **Photo library:** when you tap "Choose a photo," you pick one picture using your phone's own picker. The app receives only the picture you choose, and a copy is saved inside the app on your device.
- These pictures are never uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere.

You can say no to the camera permission and still use the whole app with the built-in pictures.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

1st Then is designed for families, including children with disabilities. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children. Children's mode has no text boxes, no links to outside websites, no ads, and no purchases.

---

## 5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your information, because we do not have it. The app contains no third-party software that collects data.

---

## 6. Backups

Your phone's own backup service (such as Android backup or iCloud backup) is controlled by your device settings and your account with Google or Apple, not by us. On Android, 1st Then turns off app backup, so its data is not included in Android's automatic backups. On iPhone and iPad, a device backup that you have turned on may include the app's data. Apple's and Google's privacy policies describe how they handle backups.

---

## 7. Deleting Your Information

You are in control of everything the app stores:

- To delete a routine, open Settings in the app and choose Delete next to it. You will be asked to type "delete" to confirm.
- To remove all of the app's information, including any pictures you added, uninstall the app. Uninstalling deletes everything the app saved on your device.

---

## 8. Security

Because your information stays on your device and the app never connects to the internet, it is not exposed to anyone through the app. The information is protected by your device's own security, such as your screen lock. We recommend keeping a screen lock turned on.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, such as adding a feature that connects to the internet, we will update this policy and the date at the top before releasing the change.
